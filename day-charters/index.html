4-Hour Charters

A 4-hour charter suits a shorter window — a morning cruise, a sunset run, or a taste of the fleet before committing to a longer trip. It works best for destinations close to the marina, such as Coral Island or the Khai Islands, where the crossing itself doesn't eat into your time onboard.

Good for smaller groups short on time, or anyone wanting a sunset sail with drinks rather than a full island day.

6-Hour Charters

Six hours is the duration most families and groups choose — long enough to actually swim, snorkel and relax rather than just cruise. It comfortably reaches Koh Hong or Racha Islands, with time for lunch onboard before heading back.

This is our most-booked duration for exactly that reason: enough time on the water without turning into a full-day commitment.

See yachts for a 6-hour charter →

8-Hour Charters

An 8-hour charter gives you the most room to slow down — reaching further destinations like Phi Phi Island or Phang Nga Bay with genuine time to explore once you arrive, plus a relaxed lunch and return.

Because crew, cleaning and marina costs are similar across durations, the step up from 6 to 8 hours on the same boat is often smaller than the extra time suggests — ask us for a like-for-like comparison on your shortlisted yacht.

Further afield

Questions about day charters

Which duration should I choose?▾

A 4-hour charter suits a shorter trip or a sunset run to a nearby island. 6 hours is the most popular length for families and groups who want real time swimming and relaxing, not just cruising. 8 hours gives you the most flexibility for a longer island, lunch onboard and a slower pace.

Can we choose which islands we visit?▾

You can tell us your priorities — swimming, snorkeling, a specific island — and we'll suggest a route that realistically fits your chosen duration and the vessel you book. The confirmed itinerary depends on the boat, sea conditions and season.

Do longer charters cost proportionally more?▾

Not always. Crew, cleaning and marina costs are similar across durations, so the difference between a 6-hour and 8-hour charter on the same boat is often smaller than the difference in time onboard. Ask us for a like-for-like comparison on your shortlisted boat.

What about the Similan Islands or Koh Lipe?▾

Those routes are too far for a single-day round trip from Phuket and are realistically overnight or multi-day charters. See our Similan Islands and Koh Lipe destination pages for details.
