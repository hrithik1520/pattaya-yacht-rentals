Yacht rental prices, explained clearly.

The same group can receive different quotes depending on the yacht, charter hours, date, route and onboard services. Use this guide to understand the total, then request a quote for a specific boat and day.

What changes the price?

The boat and its licensed capacity set the starting point. Longer trips and routes that use more fuel may cost more. Holidays and high-demand dates can change availability and rates. Catering, drinks, water toys, decorations, photography and transfers are priced separately unless a listing states otherwise.

What will my quote show?

We will identify the yacht, date, boarding point, exact duration, guest count and route assumption. The quote lists included items, required fees, taxes, add-ons, deposit, balance and validity date so you can compare options fairly.

How to get an accurate price

Send your preferred date, approximate hours, number of adults and children, occasion and any must-have facilities. If you have not chosen a boat, we can suggest a shortlist.
