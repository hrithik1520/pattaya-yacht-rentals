About this route

Racha Yai and Racha Noi sit south of Phuket and are a popular half-day or full-day option for swimming and snorkeling without the longer transit of the Similans.

Good for

Half-day charters, swimming and snorkeling, groups short on time.

Travel time

Distance depends on departure marina — confirmed per vessel and route.

Ask which vessel suits this route, whether it's a half-day, full-day or overnight trip, what's included, and whether route-related fees apply. The captain and operator confirm the final itinerary based on conditions on the day.
