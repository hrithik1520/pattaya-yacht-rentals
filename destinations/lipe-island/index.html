About this route

Koh Lipe lies well south of Phuket, close to the Malaysian border — realistically a multi-day or overnight charter destination rather than a single-day trip.

Good for

Extended or overnight charters on suitable long-range vessels.

Travel time

A multi-hour crossing — confirmed per vessel; not a same-day round trip from Phuket.

Check before confirming

Ask which vessel suits this route, whether it's a half-day, full-day or overnight trip, what's included, and whether route-related fees apply. The captain and operator confirm the final itinerary based on conditions on the day.
