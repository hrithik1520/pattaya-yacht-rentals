Your own yacht. Your own way to spend the day.

A private charter gives your group a dedicated boat and crew for the booked hours. You choose the pace: time on deck, a swim stop where permitted, food with friends or simply the view from the water.

Begin with three decisions

How many guests are coming? How much time do you have? What matters most onboard? Those answers narrow the fleet quickly.

Build the itinerary

A short trip may focus on cruising and a single stop. A longer trip gives more room for swimming and dining. Destinations depend on departure point, vessel speed, conditions and permissions; the confirmed itinerary appears in your quote.

Before you book

Review the boat photos, licensed guest count, facilities, inclusions, extra fees and weather terms. We'll confirm availability and send the complete price before asking for a deposit.
