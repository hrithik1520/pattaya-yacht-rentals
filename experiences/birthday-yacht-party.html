A yacht party should feel like your group. Whether it is a birthday, reunion or simply a reason to get everyone together, tell us if you want a quiet lunch, a lively deck celebration or a mix of both.

Plan a birthday charter

Plan the essentials

Confirm your guest list first, including children. Choose the hours and whether you want a swim stop. Check shaded seating, toilets, music setup and food service on the actual boat. If you want a cake, décor, photographer or special menu, request these early so they appear in the quote.

Keep the price clear

We'll separate the charter fee from catering, decorations, water activities, transfers and any required fees. Ask for the final total before sharing payment with your group.
