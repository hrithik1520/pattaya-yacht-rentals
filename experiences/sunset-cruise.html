Make time for the best part of the evening.

A private sunset charter is a simple way to mark an anniversary, proposal or evening with friends. Choose a boat that suits your group, allow enough time for boarding and the return journey, and let us check the right departure window for your date.

What to consider

Sunset time changes through the year, and route choice depends on the boat and sea conditions. Some boats offer shaded or indoor seating; food, drinks and décor vary. We'll confirm the actual schedule and onboard options in writing.
