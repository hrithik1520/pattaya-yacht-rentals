Privacy policy.

This page explains what happens to the information you share when you send a charter enquiry.

Data controller

Pattaya Yacht Rentals is responsible for the information collected through this site.

Enquiry data we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect: your travel date and flexibility, adult/child guest count, preferred duration and occasion, boat or destination of interest, budget range, name, WhatsApp number, email (optional), and any notes you add.

Third-party services

Enquiries are followed up by WhatsApp and email. We do not sell or share your details with unrelated third parties.

Purpose of processing

We use your enquiry details to check availability, prepare a quote, and confirm a booking with you.

Retention

We keep enquiry and booking details for as long as needed to handle your charter and any related follow-up, then delete them when no longer needed.

International transfer

Your data is handled within Thailand, where our team and the vessels you're enquiring about are based.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about your enquiry by contacting us using the details below.

Contact

Email info@yacht-charters-phuket.com for any privacy question or request.
