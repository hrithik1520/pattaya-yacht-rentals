Good yacht days start with good planning.

Pattaya Yacht Rentals helps guests choose and arrange private yacht experiences in Pattaya. We begin with your group and your plans, then check suitable boats, clear pricing and the practical details that make boarding day easier.

How we work

We ask the right questions, share suitable options, confirm each boat's particulars and put the commercial terms in writing. Your booking is confirmed only after availability and payment terms are agreed.

The people and boats behind the trip

Our fleet is based at Ao Po Grand Marina, Phuket, with captains and crew assigned per vessel. Every boat listed is one we operate or directly partner with — we don't list boats we don't have direct access to confirm. Team profiles, certifications and insurance details are shared with guests as part of the booking confirmation.
